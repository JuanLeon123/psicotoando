# Recursos visuales provisionales

Las fotografías se generaron con la herramienta integrada `image_gen` para aproximar el mockup. Se exportaron a WebP con `cwebp` para servirlas desde el propio proyecto. No son fotografías de productos reales ni los archivos originales de la referencia.

| Archivo final                     | Uso                          |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| `public/images/family-play.webp`  | Portada, 1448 × 1086         |
| `public/images/kit-sensory.webp`  | Kit sensorial, 640 × 640     |
| `public/images/kit-motor.webp`    | Kit de motricidad, 640 × 640 |
| `public/images/kit-language.webp` | Kit de lenguaje, 640 × 640   |

`public/images/brand-logo.svg` y `public/images/brand-mark.svg` son reconstrucciones vectoriales provisionales de la identidad mostrada en el mockup. Sustituir por los archivos oficiales cuando estén disponibles.

La tipografía Nunito se sirve localmente desde `public/fonts/nunito-latin.woff2`. Su licencia está en `public/fonts/OFL-Nunito.txt`.

## Prompts utilizados

+### hero

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: homepage hero photograph for a Spanish family play and child development brand. Generate one landscape photograph, 4:3 composition. A smiling brunette mother in a cream knit sweater and a happy 4 year old boy in a sage green long sleeve shirt sit beside each other at a pale wooden table, building with colorful wooden blocks, red orange yellow blue and green. Both look down toward their hands and blocks. Cozy bright home playroom, blurred light wood shelves and a small pastel rainbow picture behind them. Natural daylight, warm neutral cream interior, candid professional lifestyle photography. Medium shot includes their heads and the tabletop with blocks, enough margin for organic oval cropping. Realistic hands. No text, no graphic shapes, no borders, no logos or watermark. Save the generated asset for use in the local website project.
```

### sensory

```text
Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: realistic product photograph for a family therapeutic play kit website. Sensory development kit: kraft cardboard box with a simple small colorful family-and-house symbol on white label, colorful stacking cups, soft textured sensory balls, wooden shapes, two illustrated activity sheets. On a light wooden tabletop, neutral warm light grey backdrop, soft natural daylight. Eye-level three-quarter view showing all the kit contents, similar to a beautiful Montessori learning catalog. Square image, generous margins, realistic materials, neatly arranged but approachable, orange, teal, lime, yellow accents. No readable text, no watermark, no photographic border.
```
